# Sprites: sources and licenses

The originals are in `src/`; `build.py` cuts and packs them into `atlas.png`.

| Files | What | Author | License | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `src/yar-outside.png` | grass, water, rocks, bushes, trees, logs | Yar | CC-BY 3.0 | https://opengameart.org/content/isometric-64x64-outside-tileset |
| `src/pixelcity/*` | industrial, modern and digital-age buildings: walk-ups, flats, towers, offices, municipal buildings, industry | pixel32 | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/pixel-city, https://opengameart.org/content/pixel-city-municipal-buildings, https://opengameart.org/content/pixel-city-brick-apartments |
| `pixelart.py` | tipis, round huts, roundhouses, lookouts, campfire (Stone and Bronze Age) | drawn by code for this game | same as the repo | |
| `src/citysim/*` | medieval houses, chapel, clock tower, tavern, inn, market stalls, watchtower, fields (the walls are for later) | sketchylogic | CC-BY 3.0 | https://opengameart.org/content/isometric-medieval-city-sim-assets |

CC-BY 3.0 asks for credit to the author: keep this file, and the credits line in
the game, when sprites are added or changed. Changes made here: the white
background of the City Sim and Pixel City sprites is made transparent, everything is cropped
and packed into one atlas.
