The originals are in `src/`; `build.py` cuts and packs them into `atlas.png`.

| Files | What | Author | License | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

CC-BY 3.0 asks for credit to the author: keep this file, and the credits line in
the game, when sprites are added or changed. Changes made here: the white
background of the City Sim sprites is made transparent, everything is cropped
and packed into one atlas.
